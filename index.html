Support

Highway Trivia is a straightforward trivia game built for car rides — one person reads a question aloud, and anyone in the car (driver included) can call out the answer. Pick your categories, pick how many questions, and go.

Found a wrong answer, a bug, or something confusing?

Let us know and we'll get it fixed. If it's a question issue, it helps to include the exact question text, or at least the category and roughly where in a round it came up.

Common questions

Does the app work without internet? Yes — once installed, everything runs fully offline.

Why do I keep seeing the same questions? The app tracks which questions you've already seen per category so it can avoid repeats. If a category runs out of fresh questions, it automatically resets just that category. You can also manually reset from the "Reset seen questions" button on the setup screen.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Highway Trivia does not collect, store, share, or sell any personal data. There are no user accounts, no analytics trackers, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party services embedded in this app. Nothing you do inside the app is sent anywhere.

The only thing the app remembers is which trivia questions you've already seen, so you don't get repeats. That information is stored locally on your own device and never leaves it — we don't have access to it, and neither does anyone else.

We built it this way on purpose. This app doesn't exist to gather data on the people who use it — it exists to give people something fun to do in the car.

Children's Privacy

Since no data is collected from anyone, this includes children. The app is safe for all ages to use.
